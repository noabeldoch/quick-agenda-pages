📋 Quick Agenda

Live, synced meeting agenda for everyone in this meeting.

  1. Invite the Quick agenda bot to your meeting so it can join and listen.
    hack-g4-bot@meetings.quick.aws.dev
  2. Turn on live transcription. In the meeting: More (…) → Record and transcribe → Start transcription. The agenda updates from the live transcript.
  3. Put the agenda in the invite. Write your agenda in the meeting invitation description — it's parsed into a task breakdown automatically.
  4. Watch it here. As the meeting runs, agenda items and their progress appear live in this panel for all participants.

Click Save to pin Quick Agenda to the meeting side panel.
